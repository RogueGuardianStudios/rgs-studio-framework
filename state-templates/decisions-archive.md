## How to Read This File

Entries here are no longer active. They were either
superseded by a newer decision or archived during
a compression cycle because they were no longer
relevant to active work.

Nothing here is up for debate. If a superseded
decision needs to be reinstated, that is a new
decision made through the normal process.
